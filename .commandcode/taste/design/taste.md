# Taste

## Design philosophy

- Prefers a minimal, editorial, simple design aesthetic — "simple doesn't mean bad" — favoring few well-chosen fonts each with a distinct job over many decorative fonts, in service of brand identity, consistency, and symmetry across products. Confidence: 0.8
- Judges font systems by whether each family has a clearly separate role (e.g., UI sans vs. editorial serif vs. display serif) rather than by raw count of fonts. Confidence: 0.7
- The editorial serif is the non-negotiable brand anchor — when consolidating typography, the editorial font must be kept intact; other families (e.g., a display serif) can be dropped, but not the editorial voice. Confidence: 0.8
- Settled on a two-font brand system with strict role separation: the simple sans (Inter) carries ALL everyday content — UI chrome, body text, chat transcripts, note bodies — and a single editorial serif (Instrument Serif) is reserved for short display moments only (page titles, dialog headers). Everyday reading must not set in a serif; serif is a display statement, not a reading face. Confidence: 0.9
